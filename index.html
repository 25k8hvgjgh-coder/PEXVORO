05 / FAQ

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Does the studio generate real videos right now?

Not yet. This version includes the redesigned interface and a local preview. A secure video/image provider and server-side job handling must be connected before it can render real media.

Can I buy a membership?

No. Prices and credits are proposals. Checkout stays disabled until billing, provider costs, and credit accounting are verified.

How will credits be calculated?

Credits should reflect the model, duration, resolution, processing, failed jobs, storage, and a sustainable margin. Final cost should be shown before generation.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. This preview uses local browser file previews only. Production needs private storage, signed access, retention controls, and deletion policies.
