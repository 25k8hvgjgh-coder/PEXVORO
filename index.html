Put ReconFeed on your Home Screen

Android phone

For the full native app, download the Android beta above. If Android does not place ReconFeed on your Home screen automatically, open your app drawer, press and hold the ReconFeed icon, then drag it to the Home screen. To save this website as a shortcut instead, open it in Chrome, tap ⋮, then choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen.”

iPhone or iPad

The native iOS beta is not available yet. To add this website shortcut, open this page in Safari, tap Share, then choose “Add to Home Screen.” This is a shortcut to the website, not the native iPhone app.

Beta availability depends on device and build status. Android APKs do not install on iPhone.
