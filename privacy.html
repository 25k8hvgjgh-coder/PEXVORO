Privacy Policy

Draft for business review before launch. This page describes the intended handling of information submitted through the PEXVORO website. The business owner must verify it matches actual practices and complete the contact and retention details before publishing.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Information you submit

If you submit a project inquiry, we may collect your name, business name, email address, business type, workflow description, software preferences, preferred contact method, budget range, package interest, and your consent to be contacted about the inquiry. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, or other sensitive personal information.

How we use information

We use inquiry information to review your request, evaluate potential services, respond to you, and maintain appropriate business records. Do not claim or assume an automated marketing subscription from submitting a project inquiry.

Service providers and storage

When configured, project inquiry data may be stored using a database service provider and processed by hosting infrastructure needed to operate the website. The business owner should identify the providers actually in use and review their terms and security settings before launch. We do not intentionally expose private service credentials in browser code.

Retention and security

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably needed for the inquiry, business records, legal obligations, and dispute resolution. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. The business owner must set an actual retention schedule and access controls before launch.

Your choices

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted, subject to applicable legal recordkeeping requirements. Before publishing this policy, the business owner must add and verify a monitored contact method for privacy requests.

Children

This website is intended for business inquiries and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when actual business practices or applicable requirements change. The date above should be updated when the policy is revised.

Launch requirement: This is a starter draft, not legal advice. Confirm the responsible legal business name, a real privacy contact, providers, retention practices, applicable state privacy requirements, and any additional disclosures required for the business before making this page public.
