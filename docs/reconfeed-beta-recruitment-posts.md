## Beta outreach guidelines
- Never claim App Store or Play Store availability until actual store listings exist.
- State that participation is free, voluntary and not paid.
- Do not represent potential features as shipped or claim results that have not been measured.
- Respect each social network's self-promotion and community guidelines.
- Do not use purchased email lists, scraped contacts, or unsolicited bulk DMs.
- Start with organic, opt-in posts; share the link only to relevant groups that permit recruitment.
- Track which sources produce genuine testers and measure real feedback rather than views alone.
- Obtain proper consent before mailing people who joined the list.
- Invite testers to use the in-app research survey and send notes on actual bugs.
