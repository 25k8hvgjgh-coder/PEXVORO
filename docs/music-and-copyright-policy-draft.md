# ReconFeed Music and Copyright Policy — Internal Draft

**Not approved for publication.** Have counsel adapt this to ReconFeed's actual business, jurisdictions, hosting setup, and applicable law.

## Product rule
Only tracks marked `approved` in the internal music catalog may appear in the music picker. Availability must be checked against the user's territory, current date, and intended use. Tracks with missing documentation, expired rights, disputes, or restrictions must be blocked.

## Rights-holder complaints
Provide a public copyright contact and a clear form/email process. Capture:
- complainant identity and contact information;
- identification of the copyrighted work;
- the specific ReconFeed URL(s) or post ID(s) at issue;
- good-faith and accuracy statements required by the applicable process;
- date/time received, reviewer, decision, notices, and action taken.

Promptly route notices to a trained reviewer. Preserve a record of the decision. Where legally required, send appropriate notices, provide a counter-notice process, and restore content only when the applicable legal process permits. Do not promise that every complaint will result in immediate removal without review, but disable a catalog track immediately when its authorization is credibly disputed or withdrawn while the issue is assessed.

## Repeat infringement
Adopt and enforce a documented repeat-infringer policy consistent with applicable law and the platform's actual operations. Provide warnings, strikes, suspension, and termination criteria; keep consistent records. Counsel must confirm whether and how U.S. DMCA safe-harbor procedures apply, including agent designation and registration if appropriate.

## Track expiration or termination
When a license expires or is terminated:
1. Stop new selections immediately.
2. Block new uses in affected territories.
3. Identify posts that use the track.
4. Follow the contract and legal advice for existing posts, muting/replacing audio, geo-restricting, or removing affected content.
5. Keep the usage and takedown audit trail.
6. Do not leave old videos online by default unless the license permits it.

## User-facing terms to cover
- Users may only select music made available in the in-app catalog for that use.
- Users may not upload or distribute music they lack rights to use.
- ReconFeed may mute, restrict, or remove content to comply with rights and legal obligations.
- Music availability can change by territory and over time.
- Users must not bypass restrictions or re-upload blocked tracks.
- Explain complaint/reporting routes and consequences for repeated infringement.
