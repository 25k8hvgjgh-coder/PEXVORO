# PEXVORO Community and Feed Policy

## Product positioning
PEXVORO's default discovery experience prioritizes conservative creators and communities, including Republican perspectives, civic discussion, faith, outdoors, small business, and lawful firearms sports. Users may customize their interests and mute topics, keywords, or creators. Political viewpoint alone is not a violation.

## Political content
- Do not remove or penalize a post solely because it positively or negatively mentions Democrats, Republicans, liberals, conservatives, or another political viewpoint.
- Recommendations may reflect the platform's conservative-first positioning and the user's chosen preferences.
- Enforce behavior-based rules consistently: threats, targeted harassment, doxxing, impersonation, spam, and calls for imminent violence may be restricted regardless of viewpoint.
- Explain enforcement decisions and provide an appeal path.

## Firearms and explosives
PEXVORO may host lawful firearms discussion, sporting, collecting, news, history, and safety education. Content remains subject to applicable law and provider requirements. Do not allow threats, instructions intended to facilitate violent wrongdoing, construction or deployment instructions for explosives, or content promoting imminent violence. Illegal sales and transactions involving regulated goods are not permitted through the marketplace.

## Feed controls
The intended feed settings include:
- Conservative-first recommendations by default.
- User-controlled interests and topic/keyword mutes.
- Followed-creator and discovery feeds.
- Reporting and blocking tools.
These settings must be implemented in product UI and server-side systems before being represented as live features.

## Age and privacy
Signup requires a birthday and an attestation that the user is at least 18. This is a self-declared age gate, not verified age. Collect only necessary data, protect it, and define retention and deletion practices before public launch.

## Marketplace and LIVE
Marketplace listings, payment flows, and LIVE streams require separate operational rules, moderation, abuse reporting, legal review, and compliance with applicable law and third-party provider terms before launch.

## Status
This document records product direction. It is not evidence that every listed control is implemented or that the app is ready for public release.
