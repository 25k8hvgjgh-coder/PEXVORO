BETA TESTER FIELD GUIDELINES

Please review these before you enlist. ReconFeed is early beta software, so your honest feedback matters more than polished posts.

  1. Test the basics: create and confirm an account, sign in, explore the feed, try posts, messages, and marketplace features that are available on your device.
  2. Report issues clearly: include your device, browser or app version, steps to reproduce, expected behavior, and what actually happened. Don't share passwords or private account details.
  3. Respect everyone: no harassment, threats, doxxing, scams, hate, or illegal activity. Veteran-owned does not mean veterans-only.
  4. Respect privacy: ask permission before sharing photos, messages, screenshots, or another person's personal information. Use test content where practical.
  5. Be patient with beta software: features may be incomplete; uploads, notifications and playback may fail. Report bugs instead of relying on the app for emergencies or important data.
  6. Be genuine: testers are unpaid volunteers, 18+. Participation is free, optional, and isn't a guarantee of a native iPhone beta invitation.
