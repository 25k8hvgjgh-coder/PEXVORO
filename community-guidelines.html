RECONFEED / BETA LEGAL INFORMATION

Community Guidelines

Effective October 10, 2026. Operational details and jurisdiction-specific provisions require final operator and legal-counsel review.

ReconFeed is an adult (18+) veteran-owned community. The rules address conduct and legal obligations, not simply agreement or disagreement with an opinion. Separate beta access criteria are disclosed before registration.

Do not publish

  • Threats, targeted harassment, stalking, doxxing, violent incitement or nonconsensual intimate images.
  • Illegal sexual abuse material, exploitation of minors, impersonation, phishing, scams, malware or fake engagement.
  • Copyright-infringing videos or commercial music without permission; misleading trademarks, counterfeit products or unauthorized use of another person's image.
  • Listings for stolen, counterfeit or unlawfully regulated goods, illegal transactions or unsafe prohibited items.
  • Content that discloses another person's private identity, address or communications without authorization.

Creators' responsibilities

Verify rights to music, graphics, footage and personal likenesses before publishing. Obtain permission for recognizable individuals where legally required; avoid unnecessary third-party logos. AI-generated output must be reviewed for accuracy, rights and safety before posting. Prominent logos, brand references or military insignia must not falsely imply endorsement or government affiliation.

Reporting and enforcement

Use in-app report features or email reconfeed@reconfeed.com with relevant URLs and description. Reports are reviewed case by case; some content may be removed, restricted or escalated to appropriate authorities as required. Users may request review of an enforcement decision by emailing support. Respect blocks and account restrictions.

Marketplace

Only lawfully transferable goods may be advertised. Users remain responsible for local laws, refunds, shipping and taxes. See Marketplace Terms. LIVE broadcasts and other beta features must satisfy additional safety and licensing requirements before wider rollout.
